# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis — Tableau Project

## Project Overview
Analysed sales performance, profitability, and growth trends using Tableau. Used the Superstore dataset as a suitable alternative to the Coffee Shop dataset, as both contain comparable data fields and structure.

## Tools Used
- Tableau Public
- Data visualisation — regional, product, and time-based analysis

## Analysis and Findings

### Regional Performance
- The West region generates the highest sales and overall profit, showing strong market performance
- The Central region records high order volume but lower profit margins, indicating higher operating costs
- The East region performs above average; the South region falls below the sales benchmark
- Opportunities exist to improve performance in Central and South regions through targeted strategies

### Product Profitability
- Profit is heavily concentrated among the top 10 products
- Focusing resources and promotion on high-performing items would maximise overall business profitability

### Sales Trend and Growth
- Sales show an overall upward trajectory across the observed period
- A temporary dip in 2015 does not alter the positive long-term growth trend
- The business is on a consistent growth path

### Forecasting
- Time-series forecasting could not be completed in Tableau Public; this feature requires Tableau Desktop
- Familiar with the methodology: forecast options are available through the Analytics pane and can be applied to time-series visualisations
